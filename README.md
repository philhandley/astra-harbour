# Jelly Harbour

*Material Studies.* A little container harbour set in a wobbly block of jelly sea, drawn with WebGPU, that is also a maths game for a six-year-old.

- **Crane Tables** (2s, 5s, 10s): the gantry cranes load rows of containers onto the feeder ship. Load (tap to load rows while the count goes 5, 10, 15), Count (how many boxes on the deck?), Quick (facts, missing numbers, sharing into rows), Line (load to an order with no counter until she sits on her load line). A finished round earns a patch and the ship sails a lap of the harbour.
- **Fish Bonds** (make 10, 20 and 100): each fishing boat's deck is a ten frame; two boats make twenty; Make 100 uses rows of ten on the big ship. Fill (add crates until full), More (how many more? how many left?), Quick (number sentences). A finished round earns a patch and the boats parade past the lighthouse.
- **Gulls on the Roof** (make 10 and 20): gulls stand on red and white pegs along the fish shed's roof; sound the horn and some fly off. How many are left? How many flew? How many more can land?
- **The harbour** runs on its own between games: the cranes swap boxes, the tug casts the ship off and pushes her back onto the fenders, gulls perch and circle, fish school under the hulls. Drag the water to make waves, drag the block's side to tip the jelly, throw a lifebuoy, feed the fish, sound the horn (Space).

No build step and no libraries: plain HTML, CSS and ES modules. See `CLAUDE.md` for the working rules and `ENGINE.md` for the engine contract. If WebGPU is missing, a flat 2D harbour runs all the games.
